Put CityFit on your home screen.

Get to today's workout with one tap. CityFit works like an app on your phone—no App Store or Google Play download required.

Before you begin: Open this guide on the phone where you want to add CityFit. The app needs an internet connection to load current workouts.

Install on iPhone

Use Safari for the most consistent experience.

  1. Open CityFit in Safari.Tap the button below. If the link opens in another browser or an in-app browser, copy the URL and open it in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.Look for the square with an upward arrow in Safari.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen.”You may need to scroll down in the Share menu. On some iOS versions, tap “More” or “Edit Actions” to find it.
  4. Tap “Add.”Keep the name CityFit. The CityFit icon will appear on your home screen.
  5. Open CityFit from the new icon.You'll go straight to the workout app.

Install on Android

Use Google Chrome for the most consistent experience.

  1. Open CityFit in Chrome.Tap the button below. If it opens in another app, copy the URL into Chrome.
  2. Open the Chrome menu.Tap the three dots ⋮ near the top-right corner.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”The wording depends on your phone and Chrome version.
  4. Confirm the installation.Tap “Install” or “Add” when prompted.
  5. Open CityFit from your home screen.Use the CityFit icon for quick access to workouts.

Good to know

Do I need a CityFit account?

No. Viewing workouts doesn't require a login. Personal workout logging may be added later as an optional feature.

Does installing CityFit cost anything?

No. Adding the website to your home screen is free.

Will workouts update automatically?

Yes. The app retrieves workouts from CityFit's published workout feed when you open it online. If the feed is temporarily unavailable, live updates may not appear.

What if I don't see the install option?

Make sure you're opening CityFit in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, rather than inside Gmail, a messaging app, or another in-app browser. Browser menus can differ by device and version.
